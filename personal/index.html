Hi, I'm Alberto!

After exploring my mobile and indie dev work, I invite you to look at my most personal art. This is the core of everything I create and live for.

Short Film Character Design Rigging

Aazar

Short Film Development

Character development and animation rigging for a personal short film project. Aazar is a figure I've been building slowly — a being made of light and memory. This work documents the design, rig structure, and early motion tests that will form the backbone of the film.
Personal Art Portrait Series 2D Animation

Moons

Portrait Series

An intimate portrait series dedicated to the people who orbit my life. Each piece represents someone close to me — family, love, presence — rendered as a celestial body: quiet, luminous, and essential. The moon doesn't shine on its own. Neither do I.

Personal Art Abstract Loop Animation

Laberynth

Six-Part Series

A six-part abstract animation series exploring structure, repetition, and the feeling of being lost inside a system. Each chapter layers geometric motion and tension until the pattern becomes the emotion. A labyrinth you can't touch but you recognise.

Personal Art Loop Animation Concept

Loops

Animated Explorations

A collection of personal animated loops born from emotions, states of mind, and interior worlds. Each one is a small meditation — a feeling made visible and set in motion. Introspective work that lives in the space between image and music.
